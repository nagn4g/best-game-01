A gravity arena shooter: fight 12 waves of enemies in an arena bent by two gravity wells that pull your ship, your shots, and your enemies. Dash through danger, clear a wave, then draft one of six upgrades — faster fire, triple spread, shorter dash cooldown, hull and shield, a pickup magnet, or piercing shots — and push on to the Warden at wave 12. Five enemy types (mote, lancer, snare, bulwark, warden), pickups (heal, shield, overdrive, magnet), synthesized sound, particles, hitstop and slow-mo, with your best score saved locally. Plain static files, no build step, no network requests.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080. Move with WASD or arrows, aim with the mouse, fire with click or Space, dash with Shift, pause with P or Esc, mute with M.
